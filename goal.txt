My goal:
- To buil a data-driven rules engine for insurance underwriting, built in PostgreSQL. 
- Products, questions, and decision rules all live in tables
- Questions must include an expected answer
- All underwritting questions must be related to a quote_id and not an application_id
- Questions must follow a specific order
- Adding a new product or changing a rule should be  data change and not a code change.
- I want to implement two evaluation strategies:
  * Model A (evaluate full application) — look at every answer, evaluate every rule, and return the single worst outcome.
  * Model B (evaluate short circuit application) — walk the questions in order and stop as soon as a rule flagged to short-circuit fully matches without needing to look at later answers.
